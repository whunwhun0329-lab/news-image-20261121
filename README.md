A single 1024x1024 poster image that sums up the world's news of Saturday,
21 November 2026, built as an entry for the make.fun bounty
"news-image-20261121".

**Date rule applied to every story below:** the event happened on Saturday
21 November 2026, or was first reported on 21 November 2026, in some time
zone. Each story was verified against a primary source's own page, noting
both the article's publication date and the event date. Two stories from
the first version of this poster (the ICJ Myanmar genocide ruling and the
Shenzhen three-leader summit) were removed after a date audit showed both
belong to 19 November; they are listed in the poster's DATE CHECK box.

## The six main stories

1. **Iran Conflict Threatens Bahrain Again** (Middle East)
   Reuters, 21 Nov 2026 — "Bahrain warns residents of potential threat as
   Iran conflict persists". Bahrain's interior ministry warned residents on
   Saturday to stay alert; the kingdom hosts the US Fifth Fleet and has
   intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks.
2. **Russian Missiles Hit Kryvyi Rih** (Europe)
   Reuters, 21 Nov 2026 — overnight Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih; President
   Zelenskiy said on Saturday it killed at least two people, including a
   seven-year-old boy.
3. **COP31 Climate Talks Hit Deadlock** (Global)
   Al Jazeera, updated 21 Nov 2026 — nearly 200 countries deadlocked in
   Antalya, Türkiye, after a Thursday draft text removed all mention of
   fossil fuels; talks ran into overtime (also Reuters, 21 Nov).
4. **AI Sell-Off Rocks Wall Street** (North America)
   Associated Press — Friday's US close, reported worldwide on Saturday
   21 Nov: S&P 500 −3.7% for the week, Nasdaq −5.3% and negative for the
   year, Dow −1.9%; Anthropic fell 9.1% after its Thursday IPO.
5. **BepiColombo Reaches Mercury at Last** (Space)
   ESA / Nature, 21 Nov 2026 — the ESA-JAXA BepiColombo mission entered
   orbit around Mercury at 19:24 CET on 21 November, ending an eight-year
   journey that began with its 2018 launch; only the second spacecraft
   ever to orbit Mercury.
6. **Verstappen Wins Las Vegas Thriller** (Sport)
   Reuters (dateline Las Vegas, Nov 21) — Max Verstappen won the Las Vegas
   Grand Prix on Saturday night for the third time in four years, beating
   Lando Norris by 0.639 seconds after a race with 35 lead changes.

## How it is built

- `panels/panel1.png`, `panel4.png`, `panel5.png`, `panel6.png` — flat
  editorial illustrations reused from the first version (Bahrain, Kryvyi
  Rih, Wall Street, COP31). `panels/panel-mercury.png` and
  `panels/panel-vegas.png` are new for BepiColombo and the Las Vegas
  Grand Prix. None of the illustrations contain any text.
- `build_image.py` — composes `entry.png` with Pillow: dated header with
  the story count, a four-number stats strip, the 2x3 main grid with a
  detail line and source under each headline, the two-column "ALSO ON
  SATURDAY 21 NOVEMBER" list, the DATE CHECK box, and a footer. All
  lettering is rendered by PIL from Noto Sans Bold.
- `entry.png` — the final image: 1024x1024 PNG, at most 1 MiB.

## How the entry program works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies the `entry.png` stored beside
it into the output directory as `entry.png`. The output directory is read from
the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable and defaults to `/output`; the
directory is created if it does not exist yet. Running it any number of times
produces byte-identical output.
